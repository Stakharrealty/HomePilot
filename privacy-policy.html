Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

HomePilot ("we," "us," "the site") is operated by Sandeep Takhar, a REALTOR® with RE/MAX Realty Specialists Inc., Brokerage. This policy explains what information we collect when you use myhomepilot.ca, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information You Provide

When you use the affordability calculator, you may enter:

  • Household income, down payment, and existing monthly debt
  • Work location and commute preferences
  • Household/family size and first-time buyer status

This information is used to calculate your results in your browser. We do not require a bank login, credit check, or Social Insurance Number, and we never ask for any of these.

Your answers are kept in your own browser, never on our servers. When you open the listings, HomePilot keeps them in this browser tab (session storage) so the listing pages can show what each home would cost you; they are deleted when you close the tab. If the listings open in a new tab, your answers are passed to it through your browser's local storage and deleted from there as soon as it opens. Your acceptance of our terms is also kept in your browser's local storage, so we don't ask again on your next visit.

HomePilot does not ask for your name, email or phone number. If you choose to contact Sandeep Takhar yourself, for example on WhatsApp or by phone, the information you share goes directly to him so he can respond.

We do not sell your information to third parties, lead marketplaces, or call centers.

Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and performance purposes. We do not use this information for advertising or tracking you across other websites.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and measure page speed. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate your estimated home affordability and matching Ontario cities
  • To respond when you contact Sandeep Takhar yourself, for example on WhatsApp or by phone
  • To improve the accuracy and usefulness of HomePilot

Third-Party Services

HomePilot uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting and infrastructure
  • PropTx IDX — provides live real estate listing data
  • Anthropic — generates the optional AI city insights. When you expand that panel, the place's name, your annual income and the amount a bank might lend you (the figure in your results) are sent to Anthropic's API to produce the commentary. Your name, email, phone number and any listing you viewed are never sent.
  • Google Maps — loaded only if you press "Show map" on the listings page. Google receives your IP address and browser information at that point.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including using Cloudflare's secure infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Children's Privacy

HomePilot is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
