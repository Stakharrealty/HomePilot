Educational Tool, Not Professional Advice

HomePilot is designed to provide educational affordability estimates based on the information you enter and the assumptions used by the platform. The results are intended to help users explore possible homeownership scenarios and compare housing options across Ontario.

HomePilot is designed to help you understand general affordability concepts — how income, down payment, debt, and commute might relate to what you can afford in Ontario. It is not a substitute for advice from a licensed mortgage professional, financial advisor, or lawyer, and using HomePilot does not create any advisory relationship.

Not a Mortgage Pre-Approval

Nothing produced by HomePilot is a mortgage approval, pre-approval, or commitment to lend. Estimates are based on the information you enter and general assumptions — they do not reflect any lender's actual underwriting process, credit check, or program-specific requirements.

Rate and Cost Assumptions

Estimates are based on a 4.19% mortgage rate, stress tested at 6.19%. Qualification estimates are educational only and do not represent mortgage approval — final qualification depends on lender underwriting, credit, property taxes, condo fees, heating costs, and program eligibility. Rates, property taxes, and utility cost estimates change over time and may not reflect current market conditions at the time you use the tool.

Real Estate Listing Accuracy

Listing information displayed on HomePilot is supplied by the Canadian Real Estate Association's Data Distribution Facility (DDF®) and is believed to be reliable but is not guaranteed. Listings may be sold, expired, or updated after this site last refreshed its data. Always verify listing details directly before making any decisions.

No Guarantee of Outcome

HomePilot cannot guarantee that any city, property, or price range identified as a "fit" for your budget will result in a successful purchase, mortgage approval, or specific monthly cost once you proceed with a real transaction. Actual homeownership costs may vary based on financing terms, insurance, maintenance, utilities, property taxes, closing costs, and other factors.

Your Responsibility

Before making any homebuying decision, we strongly encourage you to speak with a licensed mortgage professional to confirm your actual borrowing capacity, a tax advisor to understand any tax implications, and to work with a licensed real estate professional for guidance specific to your situation.
